# KineLab Regulatory Gap (R1 — not a submission, not certification)

If KineLab will eventually be marketed for clinical decision-making,
additional regulatory and quality-system work may be required depending on
intended use and jurisdiction (e.g. device classification, risk management,
software lifecycle processes, clinical evidence, labeling review, data
protection impact assessment).

R1 explicitly does NOT self-certify: no FDA submission, no HIPAA compliance
claim, no diagnostic labeling, no medical-grade claim. Research Preview
ships with experimental disclosures and must not be used as the sole basis
for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Owner/legal action required before any clinical or commercial health claim.
